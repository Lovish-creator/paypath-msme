## 2. Invoices & Traceable Legal Engine (60 Seconds)

**Narrative:**
> *"Let's open our primary overdue case: Metro Retail Stores, Invoice #INV-2026-0891.*
> 
> *Notice what PayPath's deterministic legal engine does:*
> 1. *It looks at the actual delivery and deemed acceptance date—not just the invoice date.*
> 2. *It applies Section 15 of the MSMED Act. Even if an enterprise agreed to 90 days credit, Section 15 strictly caps it at 45 days.*
> 3. *It calculates Section 16 statutory compound interest with monthly rests at three times the RBI Bank Rate (20.25% p.a.). As of today, Metro Retail owes ₹4,85,000 in principal plus ₹16,634 in statutory compound interest.*
> 4. *Crucially, it issues a Section 43B(h) warning: Under the Income Tax Act, Metro Retail will lose their taxable expense deduction for this invoice unless it is cleared before fiscal year-end."*

**On Screen Action:**
- Click **Invoices & Receivables**, then click **Escalate** next to Metro Retail Stores (`CASE-INV-2026-0891`).
- Point to the **Deterministic MSMED Act Legal Engine** card.
- Expand **View Traceable Legal Proof & Calculation Formula** to demonstrate full transparency.

**Narrative:**
