# PayPath MSME — Product Definition & Scope Specification

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Product Name**: PayPath MSME
- **Competition**: SLIET PITCHVERSE 2026
- **Product Promise**: *From overdue invoice to an organised, evidence-backed next step.*
- **Target Audience**: Micro and Small Enterprises (MSMEs) in India supplying goods and services on commercial credit terms and facing delayed receivables.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Indian MSMEs frequently supply goods and services to larger buyers under credit terms, only to experience severe cashflow lockup when payments are delayed beyond agreed or statutory timelines. While Section 15 and 16 of the MSMED Act, 2006 establish strong statutory protections (including mandatory 15-day defaults, a 45-day maximum credit cap, and penal compound interest at 3x the RBI Bank Rate), small suppliers struggle to exercise these rights because:
1. They lack structured records of delivery dates and deemed acceptance proof.
2. They cannot calculate complex monthly compounded statutory interest accurately.
3. They are unaware of Section 43B(h) tax disallowance rules that provide vital leverage against delinquent corporate buyers.
4. They lack pre-litigation document drafting tools that generate professional notices without prohibitive legal fees.
5. They do not know whether their case file is complete and legally admissible before the Micro and Small Enterprises Facilitation Council (MSEFC).

---

## 3. Product Solution

PayPath MSME bridges the gap between everyday bookkeeping and formal legal escalation:

1. **Deterministic Ledger & Timeline Engine**: Automatically determines Reference Date (acceptance or deemed acceptance) and statutory maturity date under Section 15.
2. **Statutory 45-Day Cap Enforcement**: Overrides extended contractual terms that violate Section 15 proviso.
3. **Traceable Compound Interest Engine**: Computes exact penal interest at 3x RBI Bank Rate (20.25% p.a.) with monthly rests.
4. **Section 43B(h) Tax Disallowance Monitor**: Highlights tax disallowance consequences faced by delinquent corporate buyers.
5. **8-Stage Escalation Workflow**: Guides suppliers from friendly courtesy reminders to formal pre-litigation statutory notices and MSEFC petition readiness.
6. **MSEFC Evidence Audit Matrix**: Verifies 7 critical documentary artifacts to prevent dismissal during Facilitation Council conciliation.
7. **One-Click Case-Preparation Report**: Compiles a judge-ready pre-litigation PDF dossier directly from verified records.
8. **Contextual B2B Sponsored Discovery**: Matches suppliers with relevant raw material and equipment vendors, establishing a sustainable, non-extractive monetization model.

---

## 4. Product Boundaries (What PayPath MSME Is Not)

- **Not a Law Firm**: PayPath MSME does not provide customized legal counsel, client representation, or court appearance.
- **Not the Official Samadhaan Portal**: PayPath MSME prepares files for submission to official government portals (samadhaan.msme.gov.in) but is not an official government agency.
- **Not a Payment Gateway**: PayPath MSME tracks ledger balances and payment records; it does not process fund transfers or hold escrow.
- **Not a Guaranteed Recovery Tool**: Recovery outcomes depend on dispute merits and judicial/council rulings.
